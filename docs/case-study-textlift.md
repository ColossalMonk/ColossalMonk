## TL;DR

Built and shipped an AI text-analysis suite (6 tools) solo. Reached 50+
monthly active users organically. The interesting decisions were product
decisions, not code: multi-model support, BYOK pricing, privacy-first
architecture as a differentiator.

## The bet

People do not trust text tools with their documents, and they hate usage
caps. So the wedge was: process everything in memory (nothing stored), and
let power users bring their own API keys (BYOK) to remove limits entirely.
